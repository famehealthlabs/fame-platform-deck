03 · Jars

6 oz to 2 gallons, plastic where it matters.

White and black jars run standard in HDPE, light amber and clear in PET. Small capsule jars can be sourced in glass; large powder jars stay plastic because glass at that size is too heavy and costly to ship.

07 · The Custom Vision

What your brand can look like here.

Concept renders from the F.A.M.E. studio. The emblem is a placeholder: every piece below is a format we run, waiting for your identity.

Eco-friendly refills

Buy the beautiful jar once. Refill it forever.

The brand sells a high-end primary bottle or jar a customer keeps on the counter. Every following order arrives as a lightweight refill pouch of powder or capsules that goes straight into it. Less waste to landfill, lower repeat shipping weight, and a piece of your brand living in the customer’s home.

Pairs a decorative primary (frosted glass, specialty colors) with the pouch program from section 05.
