03 · Go custom

Stock gets you launched.
Custom gets you remembered.

A mold nobody else has. A finish people want to hold. A refill they come back for. This is where packaging stops being a container and starts being the brand.

Custom molds
Any shape, size or material

Your own bottle shape, cut from new tooling. A one-time cost every run afterwards uses, and a silhouette a competitor cannot copy off the shelf.

Unique finishes
Matte, gloss, metallic, textured

Frosted, soft-touch matte, tinted glass, plus custom caps, pumps, droppers and decorative elements. Finish is what a customer feels before reading a single word on the label.

Refill systems
Buy the beautiful jar once. Refill it forever.

Sell the jar once, then sell the refill again and again. Less packaging on every repeat order, a cheaper reorder for the customer, and your brand living on their counter.

Travel packs
3–4 days in a pocket

A compact cylinder holding three to four days, so the routine travels instead of being left at home with the full bottle.

Printed inserts
The pack-in that does the talking

Dosing guidance, the brand story, a reorder prompt, folded in with the product rather than left to the label to carry alone.

Accessories
Scoops, spoons and pipettes

Matched to the dose and sourced with the pack, so the customer is never guessing how much.

Custom molds and forming tooling are a one-time investment that pays back at volume. We will tell you when your run is too small to justify it.
