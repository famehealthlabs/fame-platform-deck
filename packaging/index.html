02 · Jars

6 oz to 2 gallons, plastic where it matters.

White and black jars run standard in HDPE, light amber and clear in PET. Small capsule jars can be sourced in glass; large powder jars stay plastic because glass at that size is too heavy and costly to ship.

1.5” wide. Any length you need.
