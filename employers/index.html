04 · For your counsel

The questions legal will ask, answered before the first call.

A supplement benefit gets reviewed by lawyers before it gets loved by employees. The program is designed for that review.

Is this safe to offer?

Everything is a dietary supplement under DSHEA — structure/function products, never treatments. Manufactured in an FDA-registered cGMP facility, and every batch is tested by an ISO/IEC 17025 accredited lab with a certificate of analysis before release. Participation is opt-in, always.

What about medications and pregnancy?

Labels carry the standard advisories, including consulting a healthcare professional — and nothing in the program is positioned as treatment or a substitute for care. Employees choose products the way they do at retail today, with better-verified quality behind them.

Who sees what employees choose?

Employees select and receive their products directly with F.A.M.E. The employer sees participation and spend in aggregate — never an individual’s selections. A colleague’s prenatal pick is not information their employer should ever hold, so the program is built so it can’t be.

Who answers for quality?

One company sources, formulates, manufactures, tests and ships — so accountability never fragments across vendors. We prove quality with paper, not promises: the certificate of analysis exists for every batch, on file.
