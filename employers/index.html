04 · Policy

Pre-tax dollars may be next.

In May 2026, bipartisan bills in both chambers proposed making qualifying supplements eligible under HSAs, FSAs and HRAs — $250 for individuals, $500 for joint filers, from 2027 if enacted. It has been introduced before without passing, so it belongs in planning, not in a forecast. The brands already in market when it lands are the ones positioned for it.

05 · Start with your employees

Build it for your own employees first.

Most brands enter supplements by selling to strangers. There is an easier first move. Your workforce is a real audience with a real need, and there is no retail listing to win, no paid acquisition and no public launch to defend if the first formulation misses.

Step oneRun it internally for a quarter.

You come out with a validated product, real consumption data and a brand your employees already recognize.

Step two, if it earns itThen it becomes a product.

Wider rollout, or take it to market. Same lines, same partner, no re-qualifying.

Why employees first

You own the formulation, the brand, the customer and the data. Your packaging carries your name alone, and your program stays private until you choose to talk about it. For a company putting its name on something ingestible for the first time, that discretion is part of what you are buying.
