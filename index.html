01

The Thesis

The supplement industry boomed. Demand became data driven and sophisticated. The supply chain stayed slow, opaque and fragmented.

Problem & Answer

Brands got faster. Their suppliers did not.

Every friction on the left is a handoff between companies that do not share a system. F.A.M.E. removes the handoffs by owning the steps.

The Problem

Five points of friction

A fragmented chain where no one owns the outcome.

Fragmented supply chain
Ingredients, blending, manufacturing and testing sit in four different companies.
Long lead times
12 to 16 weeks is the industry norm, and every handoff adds float.
No visibility or control
Status arrives by email, if it arrives. You chase answers instead of decisions.
No single point of accountability
When a lot fails, every vendor points at the one before it.
Brands carry all the risk
Recalls, stockouts and missed retail windows land on the brand, not the vendor.
Our Answer

One integrated platform

Integrated entities under one roof. Data powered speed, control and continuous innovation.

Speed to market
30,000+ units delivered in four weeks, against a 12 to 16 week norm.
Real-time visibility
Always on portal with customizable triggers, from raw lot to shipped pallet.
Quality & compliance
A certification stack that opens regulated markets, verified independently of suppliers.
Scalable growth
First run to enterprise volume without switching or adding partners.
Stronger margins
No intermediary markups. Integrated sourcing holds cost and pricing steady.
